About us

Looked after, start to finish

Women's Bright Beauty Parlour and Gym is a women-only space in Shewrapara, Mirpur — a place where you can sit down, breathe out, and be properly looked after.

From a quick threading or a facial on your way home, to a full bridal package for the biggest day of your life, everything is done with care and an eye for detail. And because feeling strong is part of feeling beautiful, we're also home to a fully-equipped ladies' gym with a dedicated trainer, yoga, aerobics and personalised diet charts.

One place. Everything you need to look and feel your best.
